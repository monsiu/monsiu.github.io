PesaShift

A private, on-device statement analyzer that helps people see fees and spending in their mobile money history.

On-devicestatement parsing
2 providersM-PESA and Airtel Money
One-timePro unlock, not a subscription

Product goal

Make mobile money statement details easier to understand without asking people to grant SMS access or upload sensitive transaction histories to a server.

What I built

The Android app reads M-PESA and Airtel Money PDF statements on the device, summarizes fees and Fuliza charges, and analyzes the balance column to identify charges that are not itemized as separate rows.

Privacy and payments

The statement stays on the phone. The app does not need SMS permission or an account. Pro is a one-time purchase, using Google Play Billing on Play and M-PESA through Paystack elsewhere.
