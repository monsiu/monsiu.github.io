ClauseShift

An AI contract reviewer designed to make every flagged risk verifiable against the source document.

Product goal

Make AI contract review easier to verify. Each risk flag points to the exact clause it came from, so a reviewer can inspect the original wording rather than rely on an unsupported summary.

What I built

The review flow cross-checks analysis with two AI models and returns a risk score, plain-English summary, cited clauses, and extracted dates and deadlines. The product also includes a free tier, Pro subscription, and pay-as-you-go credits.

Product engineering

I built the product across web, Android, iOS, and desktop, including the Next.js and TypeScript backend, Flutter clients, data layer, AI pipeline, and self-serve billing.
