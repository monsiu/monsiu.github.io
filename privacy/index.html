Privacy

Your message stays yours.

This page explains what happens when you use the contact or service request forms on this site.

Information you submit

The homepage form collects your email address and message, plus an optional name and topic. The services form also collects the services you select, project details, country, budget and timeline, and optional company name.

How it is used

Your submission is used to review and reply to your inquiry. It is sent through a Cloudflare Worker and Cloudflare Email Service to my inbox. The portfolio does not save form submissions in a database. The email copy remains in my inbox until I delete it. I do not sell your contact details.

Spam protection

The forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to help distinguish genuine submissions from automated spam. Cloudflare processes the verification token and related request data to provide this check. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Site analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics provides aggregate page-view and performance measurements. Its beacon does not use cookies or browser storage and Cloudflare says it does not collect or use visitors’ personal data. The choice to turn analytics off is stored in this browser's local storage.

Questions or deletion requests

For questions about a submission or to ask me to delete its email copy, contact contactmonsiu@gmail.com.
