Test Custom RR on Google Play

The same app and the same releases as every other channel, you just get them through the Play Store. Every tester helps Custom RR reach the public Play Store. Two quick steps:

1
Join the testers group Open the group and tap Join group. This step is required, just viewing it does not count, and the test page below will not let you in until you have actually joined. Join the testers group
2
Opt in to the test Once you have joined the group, open the testing page and accept the invite to become a tester for Custom RR. Open the testing page

Use the same Google account for both steps, the one on your phone, or Play will not recognise you. Once you are in, install from the Play Store. Access can take a little while to propagate after you join.

Please keep it installed for about two weeks Google only lets the app go public once enough testers stay opted in for 14 days in a row. Just keeping Custom RR installed is the single most helpful thing you can do.
