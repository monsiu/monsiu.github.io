Custom RR New · v1.3.6

The single home for popular Android custom ROMs and custom recoveries. Curates 18 actively maintained ROMs (LineageOS, crDroid, PixelOS, Project Elixir, Project Infinity X, Evolution X, DerpFest, GrapheneOS, CalyxOS, /e/, and more), 5 recoveries (TWRP, OrangeFox, PBRP, SHRP), and 500+ devices, sourced live from the LineageOS wiki and the PixelOS official_devices repo, refreshed nightly by GitHub Actions. 03 //

PesaShift Updated · v0.1.1

An on-device statement analyser for Kenya's mobile money. PesaShift reads your M-PESA or Airtel Money PDF statement on your phone and shows every fee, every Fuliza charge, and where the money actually went. It walks the statement's balance column to recover charges that are never itemised, arithmetic, not estimates. No SMS permission, no accounts, and the statement never leaves the device. Free to explore recent months; Pro is a one-time unlock, never a subscription, sold through Google Play Billing on Play and M-PESA (Paystack) everywhere else. Live today on the Amazon Appstore, the Huawei AppGallery, Zapstore, and direct APK, with Google Play in review.

v0.1.1 shipped Aug 2026  ·  Live on the Amazon Appstore  ·  On-device, no SMS permission  ·  Pay once, no subscription  ·  PesaShift site

04 //

NanoPay

⚡ NanoPay $0.001 per query, settled onchain

Built a per-request AI API where every query costs $0.001 USDC, settled onchain on Arc blockchain. A Solidity smart contract accepts the USDC micropayments, a Node.js API server verifies each one before returning a Gemini AI response, and the system has processed 57+ live onchain transactions. The project proves sub-cent API monetization only works on Arc, where gas costs are 100,000x cheaper than Ethereum.

Built for the Arc Blockchain Hackathon. View the submission here , or try the live demo here

Aegis Autonomous incident response

An autonomous incident-response system that monitors cloud infrastructure in real time, diagnoses issues with a three-agent AI pipeline (Monitor, Diagnosis, Remediation), and executes safe SSH-based fixes on remote servers.
